NEW MODE

Mini Ranked

Queue 1v1, 2v2 or 3v3 and play for ELO. Climb the LT and HT tiers, keep your win streak going, and come back tomorrow for a new set of maps. Every season ends with rewards.

NEW

Replay mode

Your matches are saved on your device, up to the last 20. Open one from the lobby and watch it back: pause, jump 5 seconds, play from 0.5x up to 4x, hide the HUD or fly a free camera around the map.

CUSTOM ROOMS

Rooms, your rules

Hosting a custom party? Pick the map for each round, choose how many players can join, then change the modifiers. Each round can also get its own map options, like faster blocks or endless waves in Block Dash and Cups Dash. Set it to 1 player and the room runs as offline mode, so you can practice on your own.

Runs better than the original

  • Lower input delay and smoother movement for everyone else in the match.

Stuck on the download link? Here's how to get through it

  1. Tap Get access

    The Download button opens our page on Linkvertise. If a new tab with an ad pops up, close it and come back.

  2. Pick Watch Ads, then Continue

    Skip the "2 Days Trial" and the paid plans. They ask for a card. Watching the ads is free.

  3. Wait for Skip Ad, three times

    After a few seconds a Skip Ad button shows up in the corner of the ad. There are 3 ads. You don't need to install anything they offer.

  4. Tap Open here

    When you see "Your content is ready!", tap Open here. It takes you to the download site (MediaFire, MEGA or another host). Hit the download button there to grab the file.

Still stuck? Try another browser, turn off your ad blocker for that page, or ask for help on our Discord.

Got questions?

I'm an official Stumble Guys content creator. Can I play Stumble Cups?

Yes, go for it. Play it, stream it, make videos about it. Stumble Cups is a classic version of Stumble Guys running on its own servers. It's not a hack, and it doesn't touch the official game or your official account.

?

Is Stumble Cups a hack or a cheat?

No. There are no injectors, mod menus or cheats involved. Stumble Cups is a full classic build of the game, with its own servers and its own accounts, completely separate from the official Stumble Guys.

?

Do I need my Stumble Guys account?

No. Stumble Cups has its own accounts, separate from the official game. Nothing you do here affects your official progress.

?

What can I play on?

PC, Android and iOS. Download it right here in How to get in, and if you get stuck, the Discord has support.

?

Where are the servers?

South America, United States, Europe and Asia. Pick the one closest to you for the lowest ping.

?

Is this official?

No. Stumble Cups is an unofficial fan project made by the community. It isn't affiliated with Scopely or Kitka Games.
